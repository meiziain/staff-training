The 22 built-in OGG samples (assets/audio/note_*.ogg) were derived from the
FluidR3_GM acoustic grand piano renders: trimmed to 1.8 s, peak-normalized
to -1 dBFS, 30 ms fade-out, mono 44.1 kHz OGG Vorbis.
